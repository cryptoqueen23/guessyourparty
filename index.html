About

Why we built this

Guess Your Party is a free tool from Phoenix Securitas that strips politics down to policy. Instead of asking how you feel about a candidate or a headline, we ask where you stand on 44 specific policy questions — taxes, healthcare, immigration, energy, and more — and show you which major U.S. party platform your answers track closest to.

Your individual answers are never stored. Only aggregate, anonymous percentages feed the community poll results.

How it works

Three steps, three minutes

1

Answer 44 policy questions

Short, neutral statements — just tap Yes, No, or Unsure. Questions are shown in a random order.

2

We estimate your alignment

Your answers are scored against typical Republican and Democratic platform positions — nothing is revealed until you finish.

3

See the full breakdown

Get your result plus a neutral explanation, benefit, and tradeoff for every question you answered.
